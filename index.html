Business question

How did sales vary across the selected months and states, and how frequently did delivered orders arrive later than estimated?

Data Preparation · R & SQL

Before building the Tableau dashboard, I used SQL within R to query and prepare the data for analysis. I focused on delivered orders and defined consistent sales and delivery metrics.

Product sales were calculated excluding freight. Orders with unknown delivery dates were retained in the total order count but excluded from the late-delivery calculation. The prepared dataset was then exported for visualization in Tableau.

Dashboard scope

The analysis focuses on delivered orders placed between January 1 and August 31, 2017. All dashboard charts and KPIs use the same selected period.

Key findings

  • Monthly sales increased overall, rising from approximately 112K in January to 543K in August, with declines in April and June.
  • SP had the highest sales among the displayed states, followed by RJ and MG.
  • Of 21,931 delivered orders, 21,162 were on time, 768 were late, and one had an unknown delivery date. The late delivery rate was 3.50%.

Metric definitions

  • Total Sales: Sum of product prices for delivered orders in the selected purchase period, excluding freight.
  • Delivered Orders: Number of unique delivered orders placed during the selected period.
  • Late Delivery Rate: Late orders divided by delivered orders with known delivery dates. Orders with unknown delivery dates are excluded from the denominator.

Interpretation note

These findings describe the selected eight-month period, not the full dataset. The dashboard shows patterns but does not establish the causes of sales changes or delivery delays.
